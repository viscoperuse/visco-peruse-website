About Visco Peruse

Engineering Certainty in Polymer Processing

Visco Peruse Tech Private Limited is an advanced engineering consultancy and technology solutions provider headquartered in Chennai, India. We bridge the gap between product concept, mold tooling, and high-precision injection molding.

Our Mission

To liberate injection molders and product designers from the costly loop of physical prototype rework through physics-driven 3D simulation, AI optimization, and precision tooling insights.

Our Vision

To be the premier engineering benchmark across South Asia and global markets for polymer CAE, material characterization, and smart injection manufacturing solutions.

SIMCON Partnership

As an authorized partner of SIMCON GmbH (Germany), we supply licensed CADMOULD and VARIMOS software, along with certified industrial training and workflow integration.

Why Predictive Engineering Over Trial & Error?

Traditional mold sampling (T0, T1, T2...) incurs massive steel re-machining costs, delays market introduction, and locks in suboptimal cooling circuits. We shift 90% of problem-solving into virtual pre-production.

Advanced Engineering Services

From rheological testing to factory floor troubleshooting, we provide end-to-end engineering rigor across the plastic injection lifecycle.

Filling & Packing Validation

Full 3D numerical evaluation of polymer melt flow front progression, gate locations, runner balance, pressure drop, and clamp tonnage calculation.

  • Weld line positioning & angle tracking
  • Air trap identification & venting specs
  • Volumetric shrinkage & hesitation avoidance

Thermal & Warpage Prediction

Predict post-molding distortion caused by non-uniform cooling, differential shrinkage, and molecular orientation to engineer counter-distortion geometry.

  • Mold temperature gradient mapping
  • 3D Warpage displacement vector fields
  • Pre-compensation CAD model export

Fiber Orientation & Structural CAE

Simulate anisotropic fiber orientations in glass/carbon-filled polymers to map realistic mechanical stresses directly to FEA structural solvers.

  • Anisotropic stiffness tensor derivation
  • Crash, impact & fatigue correlation
  • Polymer material characterization matching

Mold Design & Tooling Advisory

Engineering review of tool split lines, slider mechanisms, hot runner manifold selection, lifter kinematics, and draft angle verifications.

  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM) reporting
  • Hot vs. Cold runner optimization
  • Steel safe geometry recommendations

Conformal Cooling & DMLS

Design conformal cooling channels closely following intricate core and cavity contours, leveraging Direct Metal Laser Sintering (3D metal printing).

  • 20% to 45% cycle time compression
  • Elimination of localized hot cores
  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) sizing

Shop Floor Troubleshooting

On-site and virtual root-cause remediation for stubborn injection molding defects including sink marks, jetting, diesel burn effects, and flashes.

  • Scientific molding parameter design
  • V/P changeover curve stabilization
  • Energy and machine throughput enhancement
